A useful first contact is simple

You do not need a finished design package. A few accurate details help David decide what the next conversation should cover.

  1. Town or ZIP

    Share the project location so David can confirm whether the address fits the service area.

  2. Room and goal

    Name the room, the surface involved, and whether you are planning new work, a repair, or an evaluation of existing tile.

  3. Wide and close photos

    Include the whole area, transitions and edges, plus close views of damage or details that may affect the scope.

Service coverage is not project history

A town page means Mr. Backsplash is available to discuss work in that service area. It does not claim a walk-in location, town-based team, completed project, customer, review, or guaranteed schedule in that town. The project gallery shows David’s work without assigning those projects to locations.
