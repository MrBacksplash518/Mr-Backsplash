Before you reach out

A few answers that apply to almost every project.

David is the owner and installer, so the information you share at the start stays connected to the person discussing and completing the work.

Do I need every finish selected first?

No. You can start the conversation while comparing tile or flooring. Share what is decided, what is still changing, and any samples or product links you are considering.

Can photos replace looking at the space?

Photos are valuable for an initial discussion, but they cannot reveal every substrate, moisture, movement, or access condition. David can explain what else is needed after reviewing them.

Should I hide or patch damage before taking photos?

No. Show cracked grout, loose tile, staining, uneven surfaces, previous patches, and the wider area around them. Visible context is more useful than a close crop alone.

Ready to show David the space?

Add your town or ZIP, describe the area, and attach a few wide and close photos. The message opens for you to review before anything is sent.
