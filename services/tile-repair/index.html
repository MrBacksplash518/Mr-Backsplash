Helpful answers

Tile repair FAQs

What should I send when I ask about a tile repair?

Send a wide photo of the area, close photos of the damage, your town or ZIP, and a photo of any spare matching tile you have. Those details help David understand what is visible before discussing the next step.

Can a damaged tile always be repaired by itself?

Not always. The surrounding tile, access, matching material, and visible condition all affect whether a focused repair makes sense. David reviews the details before recommending a next step.

Do I need spare matching tile?

If you have spare tile, include it in your photos and tell David how much is available. Matching an existing tile can be difficult, so an exact match should not be assumed before the material is reviewed.

How long will a tile repair take?

Timing depends on the damaged area, access, matching material, surrounding conditions, and the work agreed upon. David can discuss timing after he reviews the project details.
