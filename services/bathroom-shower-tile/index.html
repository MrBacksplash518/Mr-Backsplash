Bathroom & Shower Tile

Plan bathroom floors, tub surrounds, shower walls, and feature areas with one owner-installer paying attention to the tile lines, corners, drains, and transitions in the room.

Text David about bathroom tileView bathroom tile work

Plan the whole tile area

Tile choices and room details need to work together.

Bathroom tile can meet floors, walls, fixtures, drains, corners, niches, and adjacent finishes. David reviews the selected tile and the visible conditions so the layout can be discussed as one connected project.

Tile size, pattern & grout

Wall and floor tile size, pattern direction, grout color, and joint appearance influence both the visual rhythm and how the layout meets the room.

Drains, corners & transitions

Share the location of drains, corners, built-in details, fixtures, thresholds, and changes between materials so they can be considered before installation.

Preparation & access

Clear personal items from the area and send wide and close photos. Existing surfaces and the work involved are reviewed before David discusses the installation sequence.

David’s process

A direct path from project photos to finished tile.

David personally discusses, plans, installs, reviews, and cleans up each bathroom tile project.

  1. Show the space

    Text your town or ZIP with full-room photos and closer views of the tile areas, drains, corners, and transitions.

  2. Plan the details

    Discuss the selected tile, pattern, grout, edges, transitions, built-in details, and the visible work involved.

  3. Install carefully

    David completes the tile work with attention to lines, cuts, corners, drains, seams, and finish details.

  4. Review and clean up

    David checks the finished area with you and cleans the work area before wrapping up.

The scope determines the schedule.

A bathroom floor, tub surround, and shower wall do not all involve the same work. Existing conditions, preparation, the chosen tile and pattern, the number of surfaces, transitions, and other details can affect timing.

David will give you project-specific timing information after reviewing the space and the work you are planning.

Bathroom tile FAQs

Questions worth asking early.

What bathroom tile projects can I ask David about?

You can ask about bathroom floors, tub surrounds, shower walls, and tiled feature areas. David will review the specific space and work involved.

What photos should I send?

Send wide photos of the room and closer photos of walls, floors, drains, corners, built-in details, and transitions that affect the tile area.

Can David help think through the tile layout?

Yes. David discusses the selected tile, pattern, grout, edges, transitions, and how the layout meets the features in the room.

Who will install the bathroom tile?

David is the owner and sole installer. He remains your point of contact from the first conversation through final cleanup.

How long does bathroom or shower tile take?

Timing depends on the project area, existing conditions, preparation, tile and layout, transitions, and the work involved. David discusses timing after reviewing the space.
