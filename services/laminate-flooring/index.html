Direction & transitions

David’s process

The person who plans the floor installs it.

David stays accountable for the conversation, layout, installation, finish details, and cleanup.

  1. Tell David about the project

    Call, text, and send photos so David can understand the rooms, existing floor, selected product, and what you want installed.

  2. Plan the details

    Talk through plank direction, seams, edges, doorways, transitions, product information, and work involved before installation begins.

  3. Install carefully

    David completes the agreed installation himself with attention to the layout, seams, perimeter edges, doorways, and transitions.

  4. Finish cleanly

    David checks the finished work with you and cleans the work area before the project is wrapped up.

Timing depends on the room and product.

Room size and shape, the existing-floor condition, product requirements, layout, doorways, edges, and transitions all affect project timing.

David can discuss timing after he reviews the room photos, product information, and agreed scope. No duration is assumed before those details are understood.

Helpful answers

Laminate flooring FAQs

What product details should I send David?

Send the product name, manufacturer information or installation instructions, plank dimensions, and photos of the packaging if available. These details help David review the selected flooring before installation is planned.

How is the plank direction planned?

David considers the room shape, sight lines, doorways, edges, transitions, and the selected product requirements before discussing plank direction with you.

What should I prepare before laminate flooring installation?

Send room photos, the approximate dimensions if known, views of the existing floor, and close photos of doorways and transitions. David will confirm what needs to be accessible or moved before work begins.

How long does laminate flooring installation take?

Timing depends on the room size and shape, existing-floor condition, product requirements, layout, doorways, edges, and transitions. David can discuss timing after reviewing the project.
