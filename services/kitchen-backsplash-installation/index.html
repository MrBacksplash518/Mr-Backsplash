Kitchen Backsplash Installation

Give the space between your counters and cabinets a considered finish. David plans around the room’s real lines and installs the backsplash himself from layout through cleanup.

Text David about a backsplash View kitchen tile work

Plan the visible details

A backsplash is small in area, but rich in decisions.

The most successful layout responds to the tile, the cabinet and counter lines, and the details already on the wall. David talks those choices through with you before installation begins.

Tile, pattern & grout

Tile size and shape, the pattern you prefer, grout color, and joint appearance all influence how the finished field reads across the kitchen.

Outlets, corners & edges

Outlets, inside and outside corners, open wall edges, cabinet lines, and focal areas such as the space behind a range need to be considered together.

Wall & room preparation

Clear the counters and share wide photos of the walls. David will review the visible wall condition, tile choice, access, and the work involved before the start is scheduled.

David’s process

One person accountable from first text to final cleanup.

You do not get passed from an estimator to a separate crew. David stays your point of contact and installer throughout the project.

  1. Share the kitchen

    Call or text with your town or ZIP, photos of the full wall, and any tile or pattern you are considering.

  2. Plan the layout

    Talk through material, starting points, outlets, corners, edges, grout, and the scope David can see in the space.

  3. Install carefully

    David handles the layout, cuts, setting, grout, and finish details with attention to the lines around the kitchen.

  4. Review and clean up

    David checks the finished backsplash with you and cleans the work area before the project is wrapped up.

Timing follows the actual project.

There is no honest one-size-fits-all duration. The size and condition of the wall, preparation, tile and pattern, number of outlets and corners, edge treatment, and other details can all affect the sequence.

Once David has seen the space and knows what you want to install, he can discuss the work involved and the timing for your backsplash.

Kitchen backsplash FAQs

Helpful answers before you text.

Do I need to choose tile before contacting David?

No. You can reach out while comparing tile or after you have selected it. Photos and approximate measurements help David understand the space.

Can I text photos of my kitchen?

Yes. Photos of the full backsplash area, outlets, corners, and any tile you are considering are helpful when starting the conversation.

What layout details should I think about?

Tile size and pattern, grout color, starting points, visible edges, outlets, corners, cabinet lines, and focal areas can all affect the layout.

Will I work directly with David?

Yes. David discusses the project, plans the details, completes the installation, reviews the finished work, and handles final cleanup.

How long will my backsplash take?

Timing depends on the size and condition of the area, preparation needed, tile and pattern, edges, and other project details. David can discuss timing after reviewing the space and selected material.
