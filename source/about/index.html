About Mr. Backsplash

One Owner. One Installer.
Every Detail Matters.

Mr. Backsplash is David LaFaver Jr., a solo tile contractor focused on backsplashes, bathroom and shower tile, tile floors and repairs, and laminate flooring.

My Approach

The person you call is the person who installs.

When you reach out to Mr. Backsplash, you work directly with me. I talk through the project with you, plan the layout, make the cuts, complete the installation, and handle the final cleanup.

There is no handoff to a crew—just one person accountable from the first conversation through the finished work.
