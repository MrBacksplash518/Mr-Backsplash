Read the bathroom and shower planning guide →

  1. Photograph the whole enclosure

    Include the tub or shower, every wall, the floor, plumbing locations, openings, niches, and the transition to the rest of the room.

  2. Identify focal surfaces

    If a pattern or contrasting surface is planned, note where it should begin and end and which view should feel most balanced when the room is entered.

  3. Confirm the actual products

    Wall, floor, trim, grout, fixtures, and substrate conditions all affect planning. Product names and photos are more reliable than appearance alone.
